# Growth Operator Brand

## Brand Vision

Growth Operator should feel premium, calm, modern, and trustworthy.

The product should communicate confidence and clarity rather than complexity.

---

## Brand V1

### Name

Growth Operator

### Logo Direction

Compass / Direction Mark

Represents:

- Clarity
- Forward Progress
- Guidance
- Growth
- Confidence

The logo should communicate:

> "You always know what to improve next."

---

## Color Palette

Primary

- Slate Gray

---

## Style

- Premium SaaS
- Modern
- Minimal
- Confident
- Executive
- Something a founder would proudly wear on a Melin hat or Nike polo.

---

## Working Tagline

Growth isn't luck.

It's operated.

---

## Brand Test

Every branding decision should pass these tests.

Would I proudly wear it on:

- Black Melin hat
- Nike polo
- Quarter zip
- Conference badge

Would it look natural on:

- Dashboard
- Sales deck
- Website
- Business card
