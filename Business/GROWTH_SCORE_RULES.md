# Growth Score Rules

## Purpose

The Growth Score should answer one question:

"How much opportunity is this operator missing?"

The score should not measure how beautiful a website is.

It should measure growth potential.

---

# Categories

## Website

Questions:
- Website age
- Mobile friendly
- Fast loading
- Modern design

---

## Direct Bookings

Questions:
- Estimated % of direct bookings
- Booking software
- Booking button visibility
- Online booking experience

---

## Google

Questions:
- Google Reviews
- Google Business Profile
- Local SEO
- Organic traffic

---

## Trust

Questions:
- Customer photos
- Testimonials
- Social proof
- Awards
- FAQ

---

## Marketing

Questions:
- Email marketing
- Facebook Pixel
- Google Analytics
- Review automation
- Blog

---
