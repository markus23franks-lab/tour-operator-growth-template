## Build Toward Founder-Visible Product Outcomes

Markus responds best when technical progress becomes tangible customer/product progress.

Backend intelligence work is important, but prolonged stretches where the visible product appears unchanged materially reduce founder energy and momentum.

Development should therefore preserve the working guardrail:

> No more than approximately 2–3 primarily backend/intelligence sessions without producing or unlocking a meaningful customer-facing capability leap where practical.

This is not permission to build fake frontend progress.

It is a reminder that customer value — not technical activity — is the goal.

Growth Score Build 031 was a strong example:

Weeks of Analyzer/evidence work became much easier to understand and value once it powered a customer-facing business score, constraint, economic opportunity and action path.
