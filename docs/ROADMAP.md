# Growth Operator Roadmap

This document captures the long-term vision of Growth Operator.

Unlike the Sprint document, which tracks what we are building now, this roadmap tracks the major product modules we intend to build over time.

---

# Phase 1 — Growth Score (Current)

Goal:
Create the best growth assessment experience in the tour industry.

Core Features

- Growth Assessment
- Growth Score
- Business Health Dashboard
- Growth Blueprint
- 90-Day Action Plan

---

# Phase 2 — Reputation Intelligence

Vision

Go beyond collecting reviews.

Turn customer feedback into business intelligence.

Potential Features

- Review Velocity Tracking
- Competitor Review Tracking
- AI Review Analysis
- Sentiment Trends
- AI Review Responses
- AI Review Request Optimization
- Reputation Intelligence Dashboard
- Operational Insights from Reviews
- SEO Insights from Reviews
- Weekly Reputation Pulse

---

# Phase 3 — Website Intelligence

Vision

Automatically monitor website health and identify growth opportunities.

Potential Features

- Website Crawler
- Technical SEO
- Google PageSpeed
- Mobile Experience
- AI Search Readiness
- Accessibility
- Conversion Opportunities
- Website Change Monitoring

---

# Phase 4 — Business Intelligence

Vision

Help operators understand exactly what is driving bookings and revenue.

Potential Features

- Revenue Tracking
- Booking Attribution
- Competitor Intelligence
- Marketing Channel Performance
- Growth Trends
- Weekly AI Advisor
- Monthly Growth Reports

---

# Guiding Principle

Growth Operator is not a marketing agency.

Growth Operator is an operating system that helps tour operators know exactly what to improve next.
