## GO Website Engine

GO should eventually be capable of generating and operating an optimized website based on intelligence gathered about the business.

The website should not begin from a generic AI prompt.

It should begin from GO's understanding of:

- The business
- Experiences
- Customers
- Reviews
- Search demand
- Competitors
- Conversion problems
- Booking behavior
- Brand positioning

GO should be capable of recommending, building, hosting, managing, testing, and continuously improving the website.

Website ownership should never become artificial customer imprisonment.

Domains, business data, brand assets, analytics, and customer data belong to the operator.

GO Website customers should have clear transfer/buyout terms if they leave.

The website should create retention because GO continuously improves it — not because the operator is held hostage.

## Critical Product Direction After Build 026

Build 026 proved that GO can analyze an unknown operator.

The next challenge is not simply finding more website problems.

The next challenge is making the analysis **commercially compelling**.

The target reaction from an operator is:

> “How did GO know that about my business?”

The public scan should ultimately answer four questions for every major finding:

1. **What did GO find?**
2. **What evidence proves it?**
3. **Why is this costing or limiting the business?**
4. **What specifically would GO do about it?**

Generic recommendations are not sufficient.

GO must prioritize findings based on likely business impact and explain why those findings deserve attention before other possible improvements.

### Cold Prospecting Vision

The intended prospecting workflow is:

**Find operator → enter website → GO investigates → personalized Growth Snapshot → operator sees specific business opportunities → operator books Growth Review**

This must eventually work before GO has ever spoken with the operator.

The Growth Snapshot should feel personalized enough that it demonstrates GO understands the operator's business rather than simply auditing a website.

## Public Intelligence Expansion

The Universal Public Scan is only Layer 1.

Future intelligence layers should include, where technically and economically practical:

* Google Business Profile / public review information
* Review count, rating and review velocity
* Competitor identification
* Competitor review comparison
* Organic search visibility
* Search-result positioning
* Search demand
* Website technology / OBP detection
* Public pricing comparisons
* Social presence
* Website performance
* Structured data / technical SEO
* Historical/public web signals

Prefer inexpensive or public sources first.

Potential external data providers such as Similarweb or DataForSEO remain options, but GO should not become unnecessarily dependent on expensive third-party data if reliable intelligence can be assembled from public sources.

Connected first-party data should eventually replace estimates with proof:

* Google Analytics
* Google Search Console
* Google Business Profile
* Booking system / OBP
* Revenue and booking data

The product philosophy remains:

**Public data finds the opportunity. Connected data proves the impact.**

## Website Product Direction

Website generation is a GO capability, not the core moat.

AI website generation is becoming commoditized. GO's differentiation is understanding:

* what should be built
* why it should be built
* how it affects bookings
* whether it actually worked
* what should change next

GO websites should remain OBP-neutral.

Operators should be able to use Peek, FareHarbor, or another online booking provider without tying ownership of their website to that provider.

Core positioning:

**The operator owns the website. GO operates and improves it.**

Potential future workflow:

**GO analyzes business → generates website brief → builds/rebuilds site → connects operator's OBP → establishes measurement baseline → launches → measures performance → continuously improves**

Website ownership and offboarding should remain operator-friendly rather than creating artificial lock-in.

## Review Engine Direction

Reviews are expected to become a major GO product capability and sales differentiator.

GO should eventually go beyond basic post-booking review requests and provide a measurable review growth system including:

* automated review requests
* smart follow-up
* review funnel measurement
* review velocity tracking
* competitor review benchmarking
* reputation monitoring
* review-response assistance
* measurement of requests → reviews → reputation improvement → booking impact

The goal is for GO's review system to become substantially more valuable than the lightweight review functionality commonly included with OBPs.

## Product Experience Principle

Growth Operator is being built primarily for lean tour-operator teams and owner/operators.

The product must remain extremely simple even as the intelligence engine becomes sophisticated.

Operators should not need to understand marketing terminology to understand GO.

Prefer:

* bookings
* customers
* reviews
* website visitors
* revenue
* competitors
* what GO found
* what GO is doing
* what changed

Avoid abstract internal language such as “growth rhythm” when plain operator language communicates the idea better.

The dashboard should increasingly prove:

**What GO found → What GO did → What changed → What it was worth**

Visual evidence should be favored wherever possible.

Growth Operator's next major company milestone is not "finish the software."

### Analyzer Performance

Current Analyzer runtime of approximately five minutes is not acceptable for V1.

After historical comparison identifies the correct intelligence architecture, optimize toward:

Typical useful analysis:
~20–30 seconds

Complex analysis:
preferably <=45 seconds

Potential approaches include:

- parallel execution
- deduplicated searches/fetches
- cached page retrieval
- commercial prioritization before expensive investigation
- hard timeout of weak recovery paths
- early stopping after sufficient evidence
- progressive deeper investigation after the first useful result

Do not optimize the wrong architecture merely to make it faster.
