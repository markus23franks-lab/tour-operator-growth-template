# Fresh Customer Evidence — September 2026

## Canmore Adventures

A strong future GO/design-partner case because the growth problem is not simply “get more traffic.” The business needs GO to understand the economic difference between owned inventory and reseller/OTA inventory, the current dependence on third-party distribution, and which website/search assets are already valuable.

### Durable product implications
- GO needs persistent inventory economics: owned vs reseller inventory cannot be treated as economically equivalent.
- Distribution mix is a growth variable, not just an operations detail.
- Website recommendations must protect valuable existing SEO while deliberately shifting merchandising and demand capture toward higher-margin owned products.
- A strong mission may be “change what existing demand monetizes into,” not “create more demand.”
- Measurement should follow margin/contribution and owned-booking mix where data supports it, not just gross bookings or traffic.

## Whale-watching / ferry operator

This conversation reinforced that GO needs business memory and operational context to interpret growth correctly.

### Durable product implications
- Traveler geography and operator geography are different concepts. GO must reason about where travelers search from/for versus the operator’s physical or departure location.
- Persistent business memory matters because geography, seasonality, routes and operational constraints should not have to be rediscovered from scratch each session.
- Operational friction can be an economic growth problem. Capacity, routing, departure logistics or customer confusion can constrain revenue even when marketing demand exists.
- GO’s opportunity model should eventually allow operational constraints to compete with marketing opportunities for priority.

## Cross-call takeaway
These calls reinforce the whole-product architecture: GO must understand the business model and operational economics before deciding which observed marketing signal deserves action. More visibility is not automatically more growth.
